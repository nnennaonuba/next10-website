The blind spot

Each business and team only sees part of the picture.

A business can hit its channel targets, with every team making sensible decisions, and still put capital in the wrong place. When a customer can discover a product in one place and buy it in another, the value can land across a brand's Shop, its website, Amazon or a retailer's till. Few have an end-to-end view of what the activity creating that value is worth, or how much capital it deserves.

01Before you launch, know this.

TikTok or TikTok Shop? Know what Shop adds, and what it costs.

TikTok Shop lets you capture and measure demand through sales, closing the loop between discovery and purchase. The flywheel starts with the right product and offer.EXHIBIT 1.7 · CHAPTER 6

Don't underestimate the creator operation.

Test offers creators can sell before scaling sampling. Recruit widely, then find, nurture and back the few who consistently convert.SECTION 1.3 · EXHIBIT 1.9

Decide how much profitability matters from day one.

Know the break-even ROAS of every product or offer, after its other selling costs. Otherwise, you can scale a loss without realising it.EXHIBIT 2.3 · SECTION 5.1

Premium brand? Don't judge content by sales alone.

Judge what the content sells and what it teaches customers about your brand.PLAYBOOK 4 · SECTION 6.2

02Running a Shop? Get your range right before your next peak.

Choose what you push, and how you bundle it.

Ahead of Black Friday, deciding which products and bundles to back matters more than simply adding creators or media. Know what each offer earns after all selling costs before paying to scale it.EXHIBIT 3.1 · PLAYBOOK 7

Set your baseline before the spend starts.

Know your normal sales and repeat purchases, and agree what peak should prove. After peak, check what changed across Shop, website, Amazon and retail.PLAYBOOK 1 · SECTIONS 5.3–5.4

Review every product and offer through the MVM: Margin, Velocity, Materiality.

Back true heroes, reprice or rebundle workhorses, test potential gold, and stop or prove the rest.EXHIBIT 6.2

Make the next investment conditional.

Not every order has to make money, but the reason for losing money must be explicit, bounded and testable.EXHIBIT 5.3 · SECTIONS 5.5 AND 6.3

03Selling alongside retailers? Know where the profit lands.

TikTok-native and considering retail? Prove the proposition first.

Use a bounded product test to show sustained demand, then take that evidence to buyers to support ranging and stock decisions.PLAYBOOK 3 · SECTION 5.2

Your Shop sales can fall while your creators are working.

A customer may discover a product on TikTok but buy it at a retailer instead. Look across both businesses before judging what TikTok added.SECTIONS 4.2–4.3 · EXHIBIT 4.1

Who pays for the demand? Make the evidence part of the deal.

Agree who captures the value, who carries the cost and whether the incentives work. Keep data-sharing within legal boundaries.SECTIONS 4.4–4.5 · LEGAL APPENDIX
